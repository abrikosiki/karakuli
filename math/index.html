Questions? We've got answers.

The ones parents ask most before booking.

What happens in the free lesson?

Your child gets a full 45-minute live session with a real tutor. They'll create their character, name it, pick their look, and set off on the first quest. No prep needed — no worksheets, no homework. Just show up and play.

What if my child doesn't like it?

Fewer than 1 in 20 kids don't come back for a second lesson. But if yours is one of them, that's perfectly fine — no payment, no pressure, no follow-up sales calls. We'd rather have kids who are genuinely excited than ones who are just attending.

How does it work technically?

It's a video call — your child can join from any device: laptop, tablet, or phone. No downloads required. The tutor shares the game screen and guides your child through the lesson live. All you need is a camera and an internet connection.

How much does it cost after the free lesson?

Pricing is personalised based on your child's age, frequency, and goals. Your tutor will walk you through the options at the end of the free lesson — no surprises, no pressure. You choose what works for your family.

My child is shy / hates math. Will this work?

Honestly? Those are our best students. Shy kids thrive in 1-on-1 settings — no class to feel embarrassed in front of. And kids who "hate math" usually hate being confused, bored, or pressured. The game removes all three. We've turned around some serious math-avoiders.
